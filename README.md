# Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of **Superstore sales performance**, focusing on sales trends, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional business insights.

The report enables business stakeholders to monitor operational performance, identify high-performing segments, and uncover opportunities for improving profitability and sales efficiency.

---

## Business Problem
Retail businesses often struggle to consolidate sales and profitability insights across products, customers, regions, and shipping methods. Without centralized reporting, it becomes difficult to:
- Track overall business performance
- Identify profitable products and categories
- Monitor customer purchasing behavior
- Analyze regional sales trends
- Improve operational efficiency
- Optimize shipping and delivery performance

This dashboard helps transform raw sales data into actionable business intelligence.

---

## Key Features
- Interactive sales performance analysis
- Profit and revenue tracking
- Regional and state-level analysis
- Customer segment analysis
- Product category and sub-category performance
- Shipping mode analysis
